| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Key Findings on Stability
- Ginger Extracellular Vesicles (EVs) act as natural protective carriers for these peptides.
- MALDI-TOF analysis shows that smaller fragments (<3 kDa) from turmeric maintain high stability against pepsina and chymotrypsin.
- Cyclization (natural or designed) is a primary structural strategy for increasing half-life.
